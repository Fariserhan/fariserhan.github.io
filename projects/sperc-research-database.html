How I counted unique papers

One paper can appear against several members. I filtered the roster and years, then counted unique Scopus document IDs.

I selected the 20 current workbook members, included Scopus records and years 2020–2026. Unique Scopus document IDs prevent repeated contributions from inflating paper totals.

04 / Explain

Carry the caveats forward

The evidence pack summarises publication trends and overlapping research themes for strategic planning. Observed results stay separate from future intentions.

What the workbook contains

These are nonempty workbook rows, excluding headers. They include historical and repeated contributions, not unique centre outputs.

A reporting window changes the answer

The strategy snapshot counts 565 unique Scopus papers from current members in 2020–2026. The 2026 bar covers only part of the year.

Scope check

565 in the strategy snapshot. 564 in the website extract.

The difference is in 2022: 69 versus 68 papers. Both source files were checked; the document behind the difference is still being traced.

The average hides differences between themes

I compared research themes using field-weighted citation impact (FWCI): citations relative to comparable research, where 1.0 is the reference.

Biochar and biohydrogen exceed that reference. Biohydrogen has only 14 papers; neither result is a funding recommendation.

What this misses

  • CV-derived rows reflect available records, not a complete audit of every activity.
  • Theme matching uses titles and keywords. Categories overlap and may miss relevant work.
  • The available dashboard uses an older roster; its figures cannot be substituted for the strategy snapshot.
  • No measured time saving or subsequent research impact is claimed.
  • The 2022 source difference (69 versus 68 papers; 565 versus 564 overall) is still being traced. Theme FWCI uses available values, not necessarily every theme paper.

What I'd do next

Trace the 2022 discrepancy at document level, rebuild the dashboard against the agreed roster and attach one dated data manifest to every output.
