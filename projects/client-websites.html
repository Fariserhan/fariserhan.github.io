04 / Client work

Two data-driven research websites

I organised a research centre’s and a professor’s publication, grant and consultancy records as structured data, then built two bilingual public websites from them.

From a source file to a public explanation

Scroll / inspect / pin

01 / Prepare

Start with structured records

I organised research and activity records as structured data. Both websites generate pages from those sources, so numbers are not maintained separately.

02 / Define

Give each number a scope

The professor’s full publication list and Scopus subset have different coverage. SPERC uses a separate list with duplicates removed; these counts do not rank research quality.

03 / Explain

Choose a useful visual form

I drew research diagrams as SVG graphics that stay sharp on different screens. Animated routes explain biomass conversion; gears show connected research areas, without encoding quantities.

04 / Deliver

Build the public reading layer

Astro builds pages from the records. Search, filters and diagrams help readers explore the research in English or Malay.

The chart below counts the records in each site’s data files. The process and mechanism drawings further down are schematics, not measurements.

Interactive research diagrams

Two visual explanations / two different jobs

Both drawings are redrawn from components I built for the client sites. Switch between the biomass routes, then turn the research framework; each still reads correctly with motion reduced.

Motion should clarify a relationship

The biomass diagram explains alternative routes. Linked gears explain connected research areas; their sizes do not measure research output.

What this misses

  • The monitor and phone frames show locally saved screenshots captured 17 October 2026. The public sites may change independently; their direct links remain available.
  • The counts are source inventories. Some records may be filtered or hidden on the live pages.
  • The SPERC strategy snapshot counts 565 papers for 2020–2026; the website uses an extract of 564.
  • No conversion, traffic or measured client time-saving result is claimed.

What I’d do next

Reconcile source versions and attach the same scope, period and update date to every public metric. Keep the broader publication list distinct from the Scopus subset.
